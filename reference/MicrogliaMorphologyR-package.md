# MicrogliaMorphologyR: R Package For Microglia Morphology Analysis

MicrogliaMorphologyR is complimentary to ImageJ macro
MicrogliaMorphology. You can use MicrogliaMorphologyR to perform
exploratory data analysis and visualization of 27 different morphology
features, characterize morphological cluster identities, quantify shifts
in morphological populations, generate heatmap and boxplot
visualizations of data in flexible ways including at the single-cell
level, animal-level, and experimental condition-level, and perform
statistical analysis of your data.
